# All_India_Index_Upto_April23
This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) using official "All India Index" data from January 2013 to April 2023. It tracks 26 consumption sub-groups (Cereals, Fuel, Housing, Health, Transport, etc.) split by Rural, Urban, and Combined sectors, culminating in the General Index.

The workbook has 6 sheets: the master index data; "Broader Contribution," showing which categories drive headline inflation; "Y-O-Y CPI Increase" and "M-O-M %Change," tracking yearly and monthly inflation trends; "COVID % Change," comparing pre- and post-pandemic price levels; and "Oil Inflation," isolating fuel/energy price effects on overall CPI.

Together, these sheets reveal how inflation evolved across regions and categories over a decade, how COVID-19 disrupted household costs, and how energy prices ripple through the economy — insights useful for policy analysis, inflation forecasting, and cost-of-living research.
